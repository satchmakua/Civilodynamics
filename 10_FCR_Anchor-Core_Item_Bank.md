# Civilodynamics — Factual Consensus Rate: Anchor-Core Item Bank

**Building-Phase Document 10 · Instrument v1.0 (draft for adjudication) · June 2026**
**Stage-1 deliverable of Document 9 §11 · Satchel Hamilton · Civilodynamics Initiative**

> **Status and role.** This is the **instrument** half of the FCR Stage-1 deliverable (Document 9 §11, stage 1; the analysis half is **Document 11**, the pre-analysis plan). It is the concrete anchor-core item bank that Document 9 §3 specifies but — by deliberate design (§3.1) — does **not** enumerate, because fixing partisan-charged exemplars in the *canon* would date the instrument and risk encoding a lean. The items therefore live here, in the instrument document, where they can be adjudicated, revised, and versioned without touching the canon.
>
> **It is a draft, not a frozen instrument.** Per Document 9 §3.4–§3.5, the bank is not frozen until (i) the multi-perspective **adjudication panel** has confirmed each item's ground truth is settled and documentable and ruled on directional balance, and (ii) every item has passed **cognitive pretesting** (comprehension, unintended cueing, answerability). This document is the input to those two steps. Items flagged **`[PANEL]`** carry specific adjudication questions. Nothing here has been fielded; there are no responses and no FCR values.
>
> **Scope.** This document contains only the **anchor core** (the fixed, longitudinally-comparable set, $\mathcal{A}$, Document 9 §3.2). The rotating pool is generated per wave under the same rules and is embargoed until its wave is fielded (§9). Construction defers to Document 9 §2–§5; release discipline follows Document 4 §9.

---

## 1. What an item must satisfy (recap)

Each item obeys the Document 9 §3.3 rules, reproduced here as the acceptance checklist the adjudication panel applies:

1. **Verifiable and settled** — a single documentable ground truth from an authoritative source (§3); no predictions, no attributions of cause, no values or normative content.
2. **Directional / checkable framing** — for continuous quantities, ask a *direction* or a *coarse magnitude bracket*, never a point estimate, so "correct" is unambiguous and robust to data revision.
3. **Directional balance** — across the battery the correct answers favor no partition (§4); balance is audited, not assumed.
4. **Neutral wording** — no partisan cue words, loaded framing, or affect-laden terms; controlled reading level.
5. **Explicit "not sure"** on every item (Document 9 §5.1).

---

## 2. Response and scoring conventions

- **Response options.** Every item offers its substantive options **plus "Not sure."** Directional items use *increased / decreased / stayed about the same*; magnitude items use coarse brackets; factual items use the stated choices or true/false.
- **Scoring key.** Each item has a coded correct answer (the *key*). For **live-series** items the key is **wave-adjudicated** — set each wave from the named source at a frozen retrieval date (Document 9 §3.2, §3.4) — so the question template is fixed while the truth is re-verified. The "current key" column below states the direction at a recent vintage *for illustration only*; it is re-checked each wave and is **estimated**, not a fielded result.
- **"Not sure."** Excluded from the correctness denominator by default (Document 9 §5.1); the share choosing it is tracked as the **uncertainty rate**. The explicit option suppresses blind guessing; a guessing correction is reported as sensitivity (Document 11).
- **Per-item statistics.** From responses the pipeline computes $c_{g,k}$ (per-group correct rate), the gap $\Delta_k$, and thence $\textit{FCR}_\theta$, $\textit{FCR}_c$, $\textit{XP}$, $\textit{ACC}$ (Document 9 §2, §4, §5).

**Cut codes (the balance ledger, §4).** Each charged item is tagged by which prior its correct answer typically *cuts against*: **R** (challenges a right-leaning prior), **L** (challenges a left-leaning prior), **X** (cross-partisan — both sides tend to err, or a science-literacy item), **S** (swing — incumbent-dependent; the cut flips with which party holds power, so these are the prime expressive-responding items of Document 9 §4 and balance over time by construction).

## 5. Directional-balance ledger (the §3.3 audit)

The mandatory balance check (Document 9 §3.3). Counts over the charged set (Table B, 28 items):

| Cut | Meaning | Items | Count |
|-----|---------|-------|-------|
| **R** | Challenges a right-leaning prior | B05, B11, B16, B19, B21, B22 | 6 |
| **L** | Challenges a left-leaning prior | B06, B12, B20, B23, B24, B26, B28 | 7 |
| **S** | Swing (incumbent-dependent; flips over time) | B01, B02, B03, B04 | 4 |
| **X** | Cross-partisan / science-literacy | B07, B08, B09, B10, B13, B14, B15, B17, B18, B25, B27 | 11 |

**Reading.** Fixed-direction partisan items are near-parity (6 R / 7 L); the small imbalance is corrected at freeze by promoting one rotating-pool R-cut item into the core or balancing within the rotating pool each wave. The 4 **S** items are the designated expressive-responding probes (Document 9 §4): their cut flips with the incumbent party, so over a full political cycle they contribute no net lean, and the gap between their belief-arm and incentivized-arm answers is expected to be the largest single contributor to $\textit{XP}$.

> **Balance is enforced per wave, not just per bank.** Because **S** items and some live-series items can change direction, the analyst re-runs this ledger at each wave's adjudication using that wave's keys, and records it with the release (Document 4 §9). A wave whose realized keys produce a material net lean is rebalanced (by rotating-pool substitution) before the FCR is reported. This per-wave audit is a pre-registered step (Document 11 §7).

## 7. Wave-adjudication protocol for live-series items

For every **S** and live-series item:

1. At wave open, retrieve the named source at a **frozen date**; record provider, series, vintage, retrieval date (Document 4 §9).
2. Set the wave's **key** from that figure (e.g., the sign of the 12-month change), applying a pre-registered **dead-band** so that small, within-revision movements are coded "about the same" rather than forced to a direction (the dead-band magnitude is fixed in Document 11 §6).
3. Re-run the balance ledger (§5) on the realized keys; rebalance via the rotating pool if needed.
4. Archive the frozen key and source with the wave release.

This keeps the *question* stable for longitudinal comparability while keeping the *truth* current and auditable.

## 8. Item metadata schema (machine-readable bank)

Each item is stored with: `id`, `domain`, `class` (anchor / charged), `format` (directional / magnitude / categorical / true-false), `stem`, `options[]`, `key` (or `key_rule` for live items), `source` (provider, series, URL, retrieval_date), `cut`, `core` (bool), `wave_first_used`, `version`, `pretest_status`, `panel_status`, `notes`. The schema is the unit of the frozen instrument hash (Document 11 §11).

---

## 9. Embargo, release, and versioning

- **Anchor core published.** This document (the anchor core and its ground-truth sources) is published with the instrument, per Document 4 §9, so external critics can challenge any item's ground truth directly.
- **Rotating pool embargoed.** Rotating-pool items are withheld until after their wave is fielded, then released, to preserve the priming/coaching guard (Document 9 §3.2). A small **dark subset** of rotating items is held back longer as a Goodhart tripwire (Document 9 §9).
- **Versioning.** The bank is versioned; v1.0 is *this draft for adjudication*. The frozen instrument used for fielding will be a later tagged version (≥ v1.1) carrying the panel and pretest sign-offs. Index versions tie to instrument versions (Document 4 §9).

---

## 10. Adjudication and pretest checklist (the path to freeze)

Before the bank is frozen for fielding (Document 9 §11 stage 1 → stage 2):

- [ ] Panel confirms each item's ground truth is settled and documentable; resolves every `[PANEL]` flag; excludes any item it cannot agree is settled.
- [ ] Panel signs off on the directional-balance ledger (§5) and the dead-band (§7).
- [ ] Cognitive pretest (think-aloud) on the full core for comprehension, cueing, and answerability; cut or rewrite items that load on reading level or partisanship rather than factual belief (Document 9 §3.5).
- [ ] Pilot wave (Document 9 §11 stage 2) confirms the neutral-anchor noise floor is low and that charged-item gaps exceed it (the H-FCR1 precondition, Document 11 §7).
- [ ] Freeze: tag instrument version, compute the instrument hash, register alongside the pre-analysis plan (Document 11).
